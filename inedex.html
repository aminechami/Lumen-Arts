About Lumen Arts

Two things, done properly

Most schools teach and stop there. Most shops sell and stop there. We do both, on purpose — because the fastest way to quit an instrument is playing one that fights you back.

Every course is built around gear we've actually tested, and every instrument we sell is one we'd hand a beginner without hesitating. One conversation gets you a lesson plan and the right instrument to match it.
